## 🌐 How to host a website on GitHub Pages

Follow these steps to put your website online for free.

---

### 🚀 Step 1: Open your repository
Go to the GitHub repository that contains your website files.

---

### ⚙️ Step 2: Go to Settings
Click on **Settings** in your repository.

---

### 🌿 Step 3: Open Pages
On the left menu, click **Pages**.

---

### 📂 Step 4: Select source
Under **Source**, choose:

- Branch: `main`
- Folder: `/ (root)`

Then click **Save**.

---

### ⏳ Step 5: Wait
GitHub will build your site in a few seconds.

---

### 🌍 Step 6: Open your website
Your site will be live at:

```
https://yourusername.github.io/repository-name
```

---

### ⚠️ Important
Make sure you have a file named:

```
index.html
```

This is your homepage.
